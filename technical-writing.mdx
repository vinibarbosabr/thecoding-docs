# Technical Content

I turn complex protocols, security concepts, and product features into clear, high-signal content that developers actually read and share.

Most sponsored content in crypto is either shallow promotion or pure marketing copy.  
The pieces I produce sit in a different category: **technical depth + real fact-driven research + honest framing + utility-focused**.

That combination is rare and it is what converts the right kind of attention to your products.

## How I work

<Steps>
<Step title="Briefing">  
   You share goals, target audience, key messages, and any constraints.
   We align on angle and depth.
</Step>
<Step title="Research">
   I study the product, code, documentation, and competitive context.
   This is the part most writers skip.
</Step>
<Step title="Draft">
   First version focused on clarity and technical accuracy.
</Step>
<Step title="Feedback round">
   One free structured revision round is included to make sure everything is right.
   A **revision fee** may be applied for extra rounds.
</Step>
<Step title="Final delivery + distribution">
   Published (or handed over) according to the model we agreed.
</Step>
</Steps>

Most engagements take 5–14 days from kickoff to publication, depending on complexity and access.

This ETA can be significantly reduced according to your needs (for urgent matters) under a **prioritization fee**.
