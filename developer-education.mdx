Developer education helps people understand, adopt, and succeed with your product. It includes tutorials, guides, learning series, technical articles, workshops, talks, onboarding paths, and education-shaped developer relations work.

This lane is a good fit if your users or developers are not getting enough support from your existing materials, or if your content does not match what the product actually does. It is not a good fit if you are looking primarily for reach, promotion, or community moderation.

## How I work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Briefing">
    You share the product, audience, problem, and goal. We align on angle, depth, and constraints.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Research and testing">
    I study the product, documentation, code where relevant, and developer flows. If the work involves a tutorial or onboarding path, I test the steps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execution">
    I write, build, or prepare the material. Depending on the project, this may be delivered as a draft, pull request, published article, workshop plan, or event material.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Revision and handoff">
    One structured revision round is included. You receive the final material and, when useful, notes on next steps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Most content engagements take about one to four weeks. Workshops and talks are scheduled according to the event and preparation needs. Timelines are conservative and assume normal capacity. Prioritized delivery is available for +25%.

## What makes this work useful

- I read the code and documentation rather than summarizing marketing material.
- I test flows when the project involves tutorials or onboarding.
- I write for developers and users who need to do something, not just admire something.
- I keep the structure clear, accurate, and honest about limitations.
- I can connect content work to documentation and developer experience gaps.

Many education projects reveal documentation problems. If that happens, the next step may be a [Docs Health Check or Focused Section Rebuild](/documentation).
