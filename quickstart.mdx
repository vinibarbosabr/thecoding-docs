Three lanes, one operating model: Sponsored **Technical Content**, **Documentation Engineering** (docs-as-code), **DevRel** Partner.

The work stands out where user education, docs, builders community, and security overlap: High-quality, targeted and well-engineered content with a hands-on approach, backed by 6+ years of experienced in blockchain and Web3.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you must have:

- Requirement one (for example, Node.js 18+, a free account, an API key)
- Requirement two (for example, a compatible device, a compatible browser, a compatible operating system)

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Solution Type">
    Select what type of solution you need:
    - Sponsored Technical Content
    - Documentation Engineering
    - DevRel Partner
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Engagement">
    Each solution has a different pre-built set of possible engagements.
    Select the one that makes more sense to your needs, or get in touch for a customized solution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Briefing">
    Fill the briefing form with as much information and details as possible.
    You can also schedule a quick briefing call (15-30 minutes) or an async chat via Signal, Telegram, or Email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="SOW & Signal Payment">
    You will receve a Contract Agreement + Statement of Work (SOW) detailing our engagement.
    At this point you should make the first-half payment, signaling agreement, if you haven't yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Give people a way to get help. This could be a link to a support page, a chat with a customer support agent, or a forum for your product.

  Example: Need help? Reach out to us at [support@yourcompany.com](mailto:support@yourcompany.com).
</Tip>
