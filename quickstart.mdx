## How the process works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your starting point">
    Pick Paid Discovery if the problem is mainly documentation. Pick Consulting / Review Sprint if the problem is mainly content or developer education. If you are unsure, send a brief and I will recommend one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a brief or book a briefing">
    Send a short written brief using the form on the [contact page](/contact), or book a 20-minute briefing.

    A useful brief includes:

    - Product and chain or platform
    - The surface that hurts: docs, tutorial, onboarding, or article
    - Audience: developers, integrators, validators, users, or another group
    - Budget band
    - Timing or deadline
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the statement of work">
    You receive a short SOW with scope, price, timeline, and payment terms. Nothing starts until you approve it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Work begins">
    I research, write, test where relevant, and deliver through pull requests, drafts, or published content depending on the engagement. One structured revision round is included.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  I reply within 48 hours. If the project is not a good fit, I will say so and point you to a better option when possible.
</Note>

</Card>
