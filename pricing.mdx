Fixed packages make scope, cost, and timeline clear before work begins. For most documentation problems, the recommended first step is **Paid Discovery**: a $350 review that ends with a written plan. The fee is credited if you continue with a Docs Health Check or Focused Section Rebuild within 30 days.

- All work includes one structured revision round.
- Significant scope changes are re-quoted.
- Payments are favorably handled via **NEAR Intents**, so you can pay in the token and network you prefer.

Packages are built from a transparent base rate of **$65/h**. The hourly rate is a reference for how fixed prices are estimated; you approve the fixed price before work begins.

<Note>

</Note>

## Delivery models

Developer Education content can be delivered in two main ways:

- **Ghostwriting or guest authoring on your channels:** quoted per scope at the $65/h base rate. IP belongs to you. This is the recommended default when you are building your own content and documentation asset.
- **Sponsored Technical Content on thecoding channels:** the fixed article and tutorial packages above. The work is disclosed as sponsored. Scope can be negotiated to fit budget.

## How I work

1. **Briefing:** We align on goals, audience, constraints, and success criteria. This can be a written brief or a 20-minute briefing.
2. **SOW:** You receive a short Statement of Work with scope, price, timeline, and payment terms.
3. **Execution:** I research, test flows when relevant, and deliver via pull requests, published content, or prepared materials.
4. **Revision + handoff:** One structured revision round is included. You receive a short summary and next-step recommendations when useful.

Significant scope changes are re-quoted (higher or lower). Payments are favorably handled via **NEAR Intents**, so you can pay in the token and network you prefer.

## Bundles and long-term work

When a client contracts more than one engagement in the same SOW, or within 30 days, a modest bundle discount applies. A natural sequence is **Paid Discovery**, then a **Docs Health Check + Quick Wins**, then a content piece such as a **Pro Research Article** or **Tutorial / How-to**.

Long-term partners may receive **stacking discounts** for:

- Clean payment via NEAR Intents, including stablecoins
- 100% advance payment, where 50/50 or milestone-based payment is the norm
- Early clients who helped validate the work
- Referrals or contributions via case studies and public [endorsements](/endorsements)

Discounts are calculated transparently at briefing time. The maximum combined discount is normally **20%**, and details are confirmed in the SOW before work begins.
