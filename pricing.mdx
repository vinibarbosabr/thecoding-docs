# Pricing

> Fixed packages. Clear scope. Realistic timelines. Built for teams that care about accuracy and developer experience.

I work with fixed prices based on typical effort for each engagement. This keeps decisions simple by adding transparency to the engagements and removing uncertainty, but I cal also easily adjust for specific needs.

All work includes one structured revision round. Significant scope changes are re-quoted. Payments are handled via **NEAR Intents** (you can pay in the token and network you prefer).

## How I work

1. **Briefing** — We align on goals, audience, constraints, and success criteria (15–30 min call or async).
2. **SOW** — You receive a short Statement of Work with scope, price, and timeline.
3. **Execution** — I research, test flows when relevant, and deliver via pull requests or published content.
4. **Revision + handoff** — One structured revision round is included. I provide a short summary and clear next-step recommendations when useful.

Rush delivery (higher daily allocation) is available at **+25%**.

## Bundles & long-term partners

**Bundles**  
When a client contracts more than one engagement in the same SOW (or within 30 days), a modest bundle discount applies. The most natural and highest-leverage combination is a Pro research article together with a Docs Health Check or Focused Section Rebuild.

**Long-term partners**  
I reward people and teams with stacking discounts that contribute to **thecoding** in meaningful ways:

- Clean payment via NEAR Intents
- 100% advance payment
- Delegating NEAR to [`thecoding.pool.near`](/staking)
- Early clients who helped validate the work

Discounts are calculated transparently at briefing time. Maximum combined discount is normally **20%** (slightly higher for the original early clients). Details are always confirmed in the SOW before work begins.

<Tip>
  Prefer to discuss a custom combination or retainer? Just say what you need. Custom scopes are normal.
</Tip>
