## Dash: GroveDB deep dive (2026)

- **Context:** Dash Platform shipped a Zcash-style shielded pool. I explained the database underneath it, GroveDB, for a builder audience. Dash ordered this piece after my Orchard explainer performed well: a follow-up commission from the same client.
- **Surface:** Sponsored research article on my channels (Substack and X), disclosed as sponsored.
- **Shipped:** [A Look at GroveDB: Making Blockchains Queryable](https://thecoding.substack.com/p/a-look-at-grovedb-making-blockchains): code walkthroughs, the hierarchy explained, and where the design trades off. First commission: [Dash Orchard explainer](https://thecoding.substack.com/p/dash-orchard-zcash-privacy-rebuilt).
- **Not done:** No work on Dash docs or code. Two articles, not an ongoing engagement.
- **Outcome:** A repeat commission. [What Dash's marketing lead says about the work](/endorsements).
