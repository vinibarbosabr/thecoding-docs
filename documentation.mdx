Clear, accurate documentation is one of the highest-leverage investments a technical team can make, and one of the easiest to under-invest in. I help teams turn complex products and protocols into documentation that users and developers can understand, use, and share.

The focus is always the same: reduce friction for users and builders, and make your product easier to understand, use, integrate, and trust.

<Tip>
  Available for one-off health checks, focused section rebuilds, or ongoing part-time ownership.
</Tip>

## Docs-as-code engagement

I work with **docs-as-code** principles and treat documentation as an essential product surface.

Typical work includes:

- Reviewing and improving existing documentation through pull requests
- Fixing broken or outdated flows and adding practical examples
- Rebuilding high-value sections (Getting Started, core concepts, key integrations)
- Improving structure and clarity for both human readers and LLMs
- Aligning documentation with the actual code and product behavior
- Ongoing ownership so docs stay accurate as the product evolves

I read the code, test the flows, and implement the improvements myself.

## How the engagements differ

### Paid Discovery

A short, fixed engagement that produces a clear recommendation of the highest-value next step (usually a Health Check or a Focused Section Rebuild). The fee is credited if we proceed within 30 days.

### Docs Health Check + Quick Wins

Breadth plus prioritized implementation. I review the entire documentation surface, focus on the most important journeys, and implement the highest-leverage fixes via pull requests. This is not a full rewrite of major sections.

### Focused Section Rebuild

Depth on a defined surface. We deliberately choose 1 to 3 high-value sections, and I go deep: research, real testing, possible restructuring, and major rewrite.

<Tip>
  When a Health Check has already been completed, the research phase is largely done. In that case I re-quote the Focused Section Rebuild at a lower fixed price that reflects the work already completed.
</Tip>

### Docs Partnership

Part-time, continuous responsibility for keeping a defined area of the documentation accurate and useful as the product moves. I work in focused blocks, typically reserving 15 hours per week per client.

## How I work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Briefing / Discovery">
    We clarify goals, current state, target audience, and constraints. For larger or unclear scopes I recommend starting with Paid Discovery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access & Research">
    I get temporary access to the docs repository (and related code when needed) and study the current state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Implementation">
    I work directly in the repository and open pull requests with clear descriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Feedback & Iteration">
    One structured revision round is included. Additional rounds or significant scope changes may carry a revision fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery & Next Steps">
    Work is merged (or ready to merge). I provide a short summary and, when relevant, a clear recommendation for the next highest-value engagement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Most Health Checks take ~2 weeks. Focused Section Rebuilds usually take ~3 to 4 weeks depending on scope.

Urgent work can be prioritized under **prioritized delivery** at +25%.

<Tip>
  After a Health Check or Focused Section Rebuild, some teams also commission a [technical article or tutorial](/developer-education) to increase visibility of the improved documentation. The two services often reinforce each other.
</Tip>
