## GitHub-based Endorsements

Clients, collaborators, and community members can leave public endorsements (or testimonies) via a simple **issue form**. No fork, no pull request, and no coding needed.

### How to leave a testimony

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Issue form">
    Go to [github.com/vinibarbosabr/thecoding-docs](https://github.com/vinibarbosabr/thecoding-docs) → **Issues** → **New issue**, and choose the **Recommendation / Endorsement** template.

    Or use the direct link:
    `https://github.com/vinibarbosabr/thecoding-docs/issues/new?template=endorsement.yml`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Complete the short form: your name, role, company/project (optional), a public link (optional), how you worked with thecoding, and your recommendation text (2–6 sentences).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit">
    Send the issue. It will be reviewed, and once approved your testimony is published on this page and the issue is closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
